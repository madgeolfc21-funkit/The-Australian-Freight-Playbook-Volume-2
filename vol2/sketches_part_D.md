## D01 · Chapter 5, Breaking Down at the Border · Third Page Banner

Wide 21:9 landscape. A road train assembly area beside an outback highway near a state border: a triple road train being split, with the prime mover and the first two trailers coupled and ready to leave on the left, and the third trailer parked separately on its landing legs with its converter dolly unhooked beside it on the right. A driver in hi vis is winding down a trailer's landing leg. A long flat gravel pad, a few scrubby trees and the highway running off to the horizon. Safety orange on the hi vis and the safety flags on the parked trailer, safety yellow on the dolly drawbar. No signs or lettering.

STYLE: Hand drawn pencil sketch on pure white paper. Confident graphite linework, slightly loose like a skilled illustrator's field sketch. Behind the subject only, rough patchy shading made with the side of a sharpened pencil in visible diagonal strokes with ragged edges, fading out to pure white at all four edges. No border, no frame, no background scene beyond what is described. Three colours only: graphite grey, safety orange (#F26A1B) and safety yellow (#F2C200), used sparingly as flat pencil colour on key parts. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no signatures, no watermark. Australian setting, right hand drive vehicles.
