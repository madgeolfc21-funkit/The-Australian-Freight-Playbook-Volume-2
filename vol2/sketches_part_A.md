## A01 · Who This Part Is For · Fifth Page Banner (Wide Strip)

Wide 21:9 landscape. Three people standing side by side with space between them, each in their own setting, all drawn at the same scale: on the left a warehouse shipper in a hi vis vest holding a clipboard beside a stack of shrink wrapped pallets; in the middle a truck driver in hi vis and work boots standing beside the cab of an Australian prime mover; on the right a fleet operator at a small depot desk with a laptop and a wall planner. Keep all three figures inside the middle band of the picture, with plenty of white space above and below. Safety orange on the hi vis vests, safety yellow on the pallet strapping and the cab's marker lights.

STYLE: Hand drawn pencil sketch on pure white paper. Confident graphite linework, slightly loose like a skilled illustrator's field sketch. Behind the subject only, rough patchy shading made with the side of a sharpened pencil in visible diagonal strokes with ragged edges, fading out to pure white at all four edges. No border, no frame, no background scene beyond what is described. Three colours only: graphite grey, safety orange (#F26A1B) and safety yellow (#F2C200), used sparingly as flat pencil colour on key parts. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no signatures, no watermark. Australian setting, right hand drive vehicles.
