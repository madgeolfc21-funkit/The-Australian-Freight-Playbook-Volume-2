# Front Matter

## How To Use This Book

Part A is for everyone. It gives every reader the same words, the same map and the same rules. After that, the book splits by the job you do. Read Part A first, then go straight to your part. You do not need to read the book front to back.

| If you | Go to | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy or book freight, or receive it at your dock | Part B: The Shipper and Receiver | Part F for rail, air and sea |
| Drive a truck, or are about to | Part C: The Driver | Part D, Chapter 3 on loading and restraint |
| Run trucks, from one to a fleet | Part D: The Operator | Part E when you are ready to grow |
| Want a number or a tool, fast | Part G: Numbers and Tool Index | The part the tool belongs to |

### How Each Chapter Works

Each chapter explains its topic in plain words, puts the rules and the numbers in tables you can scan, and points you to the tool that does the working out. The sources for each part are listed at the end of that part, so you can check any figure for yourself.

### The Tools

The book comes with 30 Excel tools, numbered T01 to T30, and a forms pack. Each tool answers one question, such as what a consignment will be charged on, whether an axle group is legal, or what a truck really costs per kilometre. Every rate and limit in them can be edited, and each tool shows the date its figures were last checked. Part G lists them all in number order.

Download the tools from the Supplying a Continent toolkit page.

### Before You Rely on a Number

Every figure was checked against its source in November 2026. Rules, rates and prices change, and some change every year on a set date. Part G, Chapter 3 lists when each one is due for a recheck. If a number matters to a decision, check the source first.
