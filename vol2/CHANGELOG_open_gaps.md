# Supplying a Continent, Vol. 2: Open Gaps

Moved out of Part G for the online changelog, as the pre publication review recommended. Publish this page on the toolkit site and keep it current.

Every figure in Volume 2 comes from a primary source or is labelled as an example. These items could not be found from any primary source after two full research passes. They are left as gaps, never filled with estimates, and the trend charts show only the years with a published figure.

| Gap | Why it matters | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State registration administration fees and CTP splits, for most states | The full rego cost per truck | T09 uses the NTC charge only; enter your renewal amount |
| Award pay guides before July 2023, and for July 2025 | The long run trend for driver pay grades | Part C pay chart shows published points only |
| HVNL penalty schedules for 2016/17, 2020/21 and 2021/22 | The penalty trend | Any penalty trend leaves those years blank |
| Toll prices before 2023 | The long run toll trend | T16 history starts in 2023 |
| Road train assembly areas outside WA and Darwin | Where to split a road train at a border | Confirm assembly points with the state road agency before the trip |
| The WA RAV network table | Which WA roads take which combination | Use the Main Roads WA mapping tool |
| Type 2 road train GCM totals | Total mass for triples | Check the notice or permit for your combination |
| Official Australian freight emission intensities per tonne km, by mode | Emissions by mode in T05 | T05 compares cost, time and service only |
| The ABS road freight price index | A trend line for freight prices | The data exists as an ABS spreadsheet; it can be added when supplied |
| Heavy vehicle crash deaths by calendar year before 2021 | The longer trend; an older series in the research register did not match the bulletin and was dropped | Part A uses calendar year deaths, 2021 to 2025, instead |

If you can supply any of these from a primary source, the tools and charts are built to take them.
