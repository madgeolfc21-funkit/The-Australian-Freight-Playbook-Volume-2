## C01 · Chapter 5, Your Load Is Your Problem Too · Third Page Banner

Wide 21:9 landscape. WRONG VS RIGHT: Two panels side by side, separated by a thin hand drawn pencil line. Left panel shows the wrong way, with a bold hand drawn red X in its top corner. Right panel shows the right way, with a bold hand drawn green tick in its top corner. The red X and green tick are the only red and green in the picture. Any forklift is drawn in plain grey graphite. Left panel: side view of a flat tray truck braking hard, with a tall upright fridge in its packaging standing loose in the middle of the deck and tipping forward, small motion lines showing it falling toward the cab. Right panel: the same truck and the same fridge, now standing hard against the headboard at the front of the deck, with two straps over it pulled tight down to the deck rails and a rubber anti slip mat under it. Straps in safety orange, the anti slip mat edge in safety yellow.

STYLE: Hand drawn pencil sketch on pure white paper. Confident graphite linework, slightly loose like a skilled illustrator's field sketch. Behind the subject only, rough patchy shading made with the side of a sharpened pencil in visible diagonal strokes with ragged edges, fading out to pure white at all four edges. No border, no frame, no background scene beyond what is described. Three colours only: graphite grey, safety orange (#F26A1B) and safety yellow (#F2C200), used sparingly as flat pencil colour on key parts. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no signatures, no watermark. Australian setting, right hand drive vehicles.
