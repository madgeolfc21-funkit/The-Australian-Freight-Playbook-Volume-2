# Volume 2 Part G: Numbers and Tool Index (draft)

The Australian Freight Playbook · Volume 2 · November 2026 edition · Nov 21, 2026

## Who This Part Is For

Part G is the back of the book: the numbers that tell you whether a freight operation is working, an index of all 28 tools, a calendar of when the figures in them change, and an honest list of what could not be found. Every reader comes here; most come back here.

Volume 2 ships with 28 free Excel tools. Every one has editable rates and limits, shows the date its figures were checked, and uses the same colours: yellow for your inputs, blue for the working, orange for the answer.

## Chapter 1: The KPIs

Eleven numbers tell you whether a freight operation is working. Each one feeds profit per truck through revenue or cost, so a change in any of them shows up on the bottom line.

&#91;embedded content: KPI driver tree for a truck operation\]

| KPI | How to work it out | Who watches it | Tool |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per km | Total truck cost for the period ÷ km driven | Operator | T09 |
| Cost per pallet | Freight cost ÷ pallets moved | Shipper, operator | T06, T12 |
| Cost per consignment | Freight cost ÷ consignments | Shipper | T04, T07 |
| Revenue per truck per week | Revenue earned by each truck ÷ weeks | Operator | T18 |
| Loaded versus empty km | Loaded km ÷ total km | Operator | T18 |
| Utilisation | Days each truck worked ÷ days available | Operator | T18 |
| DIFOT | Consignments delivered in full and on time ÷ all consignments | Shipper, operator | T08 |
| Damage and claims rate | Damaged consignments, and claims paid, per 1,000 consignments | Shipper, operator | T08 |
| Fuel use, L/100 km | Litres ÷ km × 100, per truck | Operator | T15 |
| Maintenance cost per km | Repairs, service and tyres ÷ km | Operator | T09, T17 |
| Driver turnover | Drivers who left in the year ÷ average number of drivers | Operator | T24 |

### Setting Targets

The best target is your own last quarter, improved. Industry averages vary too much by freight type and lane to be useful. Track each KPI monthly, chart it, and ask why when it moves. A KPI nobody acts on is just a number.

## Chapter 3: The Update Calendar

The figures in the tools go out of date on a known timetable. Update them on these dates and the tools stay right; skip them and every quote drifts.

| When | What changes | Where to check | Tools to update |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Every week | Diesel price | Australian Institute of Petroleum | T03, T09, T10, T15 |
| Every month | Carrier fuel levies | Each carrier's fuel surcharge page | T03, T04 |
| Every quarter | Sydney and Melbourne toll prices (WestConnex each 1 January) | Toll operator pages | T16 |
| Each February and August | Fuel tax credit rates, with excise indexation | ATO | T09, T15 |
| Each 1 January | Road user charge, when a change is scheduled (the next rise is deferred to 1 January 2027) | ATO, NTC | T09, T15 |
| Each 1 July | Award pay rates | Fair Work Ombudsman pay guides | T09, T20, T24 |
| Each 1 July | Heavy vehicle registration charges | NTC and state registration authorities | T09 |
| Each 1 July | HVNL penalty amounts | NHVR penalty list | Part C, D text |
| Each 1 July | Payroll tax thresholds and rates; workers comp rates | State revenue offices and schemes | T09, T24 |
| Each 1 July | Brisbane and EastLink tolls | Toll operator pages | T16 |
| Each year | National Greenhouse Accounts factors | Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water | T22 |
| When announced | Mass, access, fatigue or accreditation changes; notices renewed | NHVR, Main Roads WA, NT Government | T11, T14, T21, T25 |

Every tool has a "checked as at" date on its Start here tab. Change that date each time you update its figures, so anyone using it knows how fresh the numbers are.

## Chapter 4: What We Could Not Find

Every figure in Volume 2 comes from a primary source or is labelled as an example. These items could not be found from any primary source after two full research passes. They are left as gaps, never filled with estimates, and the trend charts show only the years with a published figure.

| Gap | Why it matters | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State registration administration fees and CTP splits, for most states | The full rego cost per truck | T09 uses the NTC charge only; enter your renewal amount |
| Award pay guides before July 2023, and for July 2025 | The long run trend for driver pay grades | Part C pay chart shows published points only |
| HVNL penalty schedules for 2016/17, 2020/21 and 2021/22 | The penalty trend | Any penalty trend leaves those years blank |
| Toll prices before 2023 | The long run toll trend | T16 history starts in 2023 |
| Road train assembly areas outside WA and Darwin | Where to split a road train at a border | Confirm assembly points with the state road agency before the trip |
| The WA RAV network table | Which WA roads take which combination | Use the Main Roads WA mapping tool |
| Type 2 road train GCM totals | Total mass for triples | Check the notice or permit for your combination |
| Official Australian freight emission intensities per tonne km, by mode | Emissions by mode in T05 | T05 compares cost, time and service only |
| The ABS road freight price index | A trend line for freight prices | The data exists as an ABS spreadsheet; it can be added when supplied |
| Heavy vehicle crash deaths by calendar year before 2021 | The longer trend; an older series in the research register did not match the bulletin and was dropped | Part A uses calendar year deaths, 2021 to 2025, instead |

If you can supply any of these from a primary source, the tools and charts are built to take them.
